Group programme · SoulQuest · January – June 2027

The journey to purpose, together

The same six-month journey as the individual guiding — but in the company of other seekers. What you discover in yourself becomes visible in the mirror of the others, and you become a witness to their journey, as they do to yours.

What it is about

Our world is undergoing a profound transformation. Many of us feel the call for more depth and meaning in our lives and wish to play a positive role in this change. This six-month SoulQuest journey is an invitation to discover and embody your authentic truth and your life's task. Through immersion in nature and through soul work, you attune yourself to the inner wisdom of your soul and deepen your connection with life itself. From this place you will learn to offer what is truly yours — in service of humanity and of the New Earth.

  • Learn to listen to the compass of your heart
  • Discover the essence of your true, authentic self
  • Embody your deepest calling, freed from fears and limiting beliefs
  • Open a dialogue with nature and with the soul
  • Live a life in alignment with your calling

This programme offers one full-day in-person session per month, one monthly online check-in, self-guided nature-based practices for meeting the soul, reading, recorded exercises, personalised written reflections twice a month and individual invitations, as well as a solo nature experience from sunrise to sunset in the forest.
